Solar Sim

This is a little orbit simulation I started in September 2026 as a forray rendering spaces in three dimensions. I chose an orbit sim because I've made some 2D ones in the past, and it was relatively easy to translate that to 3D. The main challenge was getting the position of a 3D object and translating that to a position on a 2D screen. Otherwise, the planets are easy to draw. They're just circles.
